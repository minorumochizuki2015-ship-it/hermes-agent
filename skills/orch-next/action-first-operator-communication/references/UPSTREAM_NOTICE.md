## Adopted concepts

- Put the actionable result or next action before explanatory context.
- Use bounded steps only when the reader must perform multiple actions.
- Make current state and errors easy to scan.
- End open operational work with one concrete next action.
- Keep ordinary conversational lists compact.

## ORCH modifications

- Activation is manual and contextual, not always-on or diagnosis-based.
- The first line may be an agent action, result, or real Authority Gate.
- The agent continues authorized work instead of making the user a relay.
- Estimates are omitted unless grounded.
- Compact wording cannot remove uncertainty, citations, non-claims, safety, or
  artifact-native detail.
- Existing authority, claim, option-selection, validation, and acceptance
  owners remain unchanged.
